# Carried files: deck

The deck engine, carried from the origin deck into `src/deck/` (T013), and every change made to it (FR-009b). Rename rows say "product identifier → deck"; redact rows give the line and never the original text. Line numbers are the origin file's. `src/deck/langs.ts` and `src/deck/slides.ts` are template-owned (T016) and not listed here.

## Files

- `src/deck/Deck.tsx`, `swipe.ts`, `swipe.module.css`, `menu.module.css`, `Overview.tsx`, `MenuCard.tsx`, `Footer.tsx`, `SlideView.tsx`, `SlideBody.tsx`, `content.ts`, `version.tsx`, `deck.module.css` and `content.module.css` from the origin's files of the same name in `src/deck/`
- `src/deck/model.ts` from the origin's `src/deck/slides.ts`

Not carried: the origin's concept drawings and their styles, its screen stills and their index, and its text modules. `swipe.module.css` is carried unchanged.
